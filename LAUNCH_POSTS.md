# Hacker News — Show HN Post

## Title
Show HN: 15 free accessibility tools, 100% client-side, no data upload

## Body

I built a collection of 15 accessibility tools that all run entirely in the browser — no server-side processing, no data upload, no accounts.

Tools include:
- URL scanner (axe-core powered, scans any public URL)
- Color contrast checker (WCAG AA/AAA + APCA)
- Color blind simulator (8 types + Daltonize)
- ARIA role/attribute generator (20+ patterns)
- WCAG 2.2 compliance checklist
- Accessibility statement generator (EAA-compliant)
- AI alt text generator (uses your own OpenAI key)
- HTML scanner, heading analyzer, form label checker, link text checker, focus order checker, alt text checker, PDF checker, accessibility monitor

As part of the launch, I also ran axe-core WCAG 2.2 scans against 52 of the world's top websites and published the results[1]. Some findings:

- 70 total violations across 48 successfully scanned sites
- 95.7% were critical or serious severity
- Most common: color-contrast (9 sites), missing alt text (6 sites), empty link names (6 sites)
- Worst offenders: WebMD (7), Zoom (6), AliExpress (5), Spotify (5)
- 18 sites passed with zero violations (Google, Wikipedia, BBC, Microsoft, GitHub, Stripe, etc.)

Everything is open source and runs client-side. The scan report is at https://a11ykit.site/accessibility-report

[1] Automated tools catch ~30-40% of WCAG issues, so a clean scan doesn't mean a site is fully accessible. But it's a useful baseline.
